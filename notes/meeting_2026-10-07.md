# PBNN meeting, Oct 7 2026

## What I did
- Reimplemented LogicTreeNet (Petersen et al., NeurIPS 2024) and trained it on CIFAR-10 at scale S (1.3M gates) and M (10.7M gates).
- Main problem: the hard (pure binary) network lost ~6 points vs. the soft network.
- Fixed two mismatches with the paper's appendix (head widths, residual init): S gap went from 17 points to ~4.
- Changing normal training (annealing, 2-channel trees, no weight decay) didn't help. A control rerun showed ±3 pt run-to-run noise in hard accuracy.
- Straight-through fine-tune worked: a few extra epochs with the hard network in the forward pass, gradients through the soft gate probabilities.

## Results (CIFAR-10 test)
- S: hard 51.95% → 54.2%, consistent across 3 learning rates (within 0.06 pts). Normal fine-tune gained nothing.
- M: hard 56.45% → 60.43%, soft/hard gap 6.1 → 1.0 pts. Best result so far.

## Caveats
- M is one run (but +4 > the ±3 noise measured on S).
- M overfits: 96% train vs. ~61% validation.
- Still under the paper's 71% on M; soft accuracy is under too, so the gap is training setup, not discretization.
